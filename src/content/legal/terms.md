These terms apply when you use our website and when you request or receive care from Heaven Nursing Care 24/7.

## Our services

We arrange doctors, nurses, paramedical staff, assistant nurses and caregivers to provide healthcare and personal care at home in Lahore. Services are described on our website and agreed with you before care begins.

## Requests and bookings

A request made through our website, by phone or on WhatsApp is not a confirmed booking. Care is confirmed once we have understood the patient's needs, agreed the plan and the quote with you, and confirmed staffing.

## Assessment and suitability

We assess each request to make sure care can be given safely at home. If a service isn't clinically appropriate at home, we'll tell you and advise on alternatives.

## Prescriptions

Injections, IV medication and some procedures are given only with a valid prescription from a registered doctor.

## Family responsibilities

- Give accurate information about the patient's condition and medicines
- Provide valid prescriptions and prescribed medicines
- Provide a safe and respectful environment for our staff
- Tell us promptly about any change in the patient's condition

## Changes and cancellations

Care plans can change as needs change. For shift-based care, please give at least 24 hours' notice of changes or cancellations where possible.

## Payment

We'll give you a clear quote before care begins. Payment terms are agreed with you and can be made by cash, bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa.

## Our staff

Our staff are introduced and supervised by Heaven Nursing Care. To protect continuity of care and our staff, families agree not to hire our staff directly during care or for six months afterwards.

## Not an emergency service

We are not an emergency service. In a medical emergency, call Rescue 1122 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

## Complaints

If something isn't right, please tell us. See our [Patient Rights & Responsibilities](../patient-rights/) for how we handle concerns.

## Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Pakistan.
