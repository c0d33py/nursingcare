## Information, not medical advice

The content on this website describes our services and gives general health information. It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified doctor about a specific patient. Always follow the advice of the patient's treating doctor.

## Not an emergency service

Heaven Nursing Care 24/7 is **not an emergency service**. In a medical emergency, call **Rescue 1122** or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

## Assessment and prescriptions

All care is provided after an assessment of the patient's needs. Injections, IV medication and certain procedures are given only with a valid prescription, and only when it is clinically appropriate to give them at home.

## Examples on this website

Readings, care updates and schedules shown on this website (for example blood pressure values or sample WhatsApp updates) are illustrative examples, not real patient data.

## Outcomes

Every patient is different. Recovery and outcomes depend on individual health and circumstances.
