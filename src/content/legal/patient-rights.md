We believe good care is built on respect, on both sides. This page sets out what you can expect from us, and what we ask of patients and families.

## Your rights

- **Dignity and respect.** To be treated with courtesy and kindness, whatever your age, condition, faith or background.
- **Privacy.** To have personal care carried out privately, and your information kept confidential.
- **Information.** To understand the care being given, who is giving it and why.
- **Consent.** To agree to care before it is given, and to refuse any part of it.
- **Choice of carer.** To request a staff member of a particular gender, and to ask for a change of staff.
- **Safe care.** To receive care from appropriately qualified staff who follow infection-control and medication-safety practices.
- **Raise concerns.** To make a complaint without it affecting your care.

## Your responsibilities

- Give us accurate and complete information about the patient's health and medicines
- Provide valid prescriptions for medicines and procedures
- Follow the care plan agreed with us, or tell us if you want to change it
- Treat our staff with respect, and provide a safe working environment
- Let us know promptly if the patient's condition changes

## Raising a concern

1. Speak to your nurse or caregiver, or call or WhatsApp our coordinator on 0308 2177778.
2. We'll acknowledge your concern within 24 hours and agree how to resolve it.
3. If you're not satisfied, ask for the matter to be reviewed by our clinical lead.

We use every concern to improve our care.
