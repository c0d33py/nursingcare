Heaven Nursing Care 24/7 ("we", "us") provides home nursing and healthcare services in Lahore. We take the privacy of patients and families seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how we look after it.

## Who we are

Heaven Nursing Care 24/7, 176-A Pak Arab Society, Lahore. Phone and WhatsApp: 0308 2177778.

## What we collect

- **Contact details** you give us: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, and your area in Lahore.
- **Care details**: who needs care, the services and type of care requested, and preferred start date and staff gender.
- **Health information** needed to arrange safe care, such as prescriptions, discharge summaries and the patient's condition. Our staff collect this directly from you by phone or WhatsApp. **We ask you not to enter detailed medical records on our website.**
- **Website usage**: if enabled, privacy-friendly analytics that count visits without cookies and without identifying you.

## How our website forms work

Our website does not store the information you type into forms. When you submit a booking or contact form, your browser opens WhatsApp with your details prefilled as a message to our team. The message is only sent if you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.

## How we use information

- To respond to your enquiry and arrange care
- To match the right professional to the patient
- To deliver care safely and keep the family informed
- To meet our legal and professional obligations

We do **not** sell your information, and we do not use health information for advertising.

## Who we share it with

Only with the staff directly involved in the patient's care and, with your agreement, the patient's treating doctor or hospital. We may share information if required by law.

## How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to care are deleted within 12 months. Care records are kept for as long as required for safe care and legal obligations, then securely deleted.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or withdraw consent for us to contact you. Call or WhatsApp us on 0308 2177778.

## Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we ever add them, we'll ask for your consent first and update this policy.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
