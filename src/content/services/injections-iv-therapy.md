Many treatments, from a course of antibiotics to IV fluids (a "drip"), don't need a hospital bed. They need skilled hands. Our qualified staff give intramuscular (IM) and subcutaneous injections, IV medication and IV fluids at home.

Everything is given **strictly as prescribed**, and IV therapy only where it is clinically appropriate at home. We follow infection-control practices for every procedure and can schedule regular doses so treatment stays on track, with no missed doses and no daily trips to a clinic.

## Plain-English guide

- **IM injection:** given into a muscle, usually the upper arm or hip.
- **Subcutaneous injection:** given just under the skin, for example insulin or blood thinners.
- **IV medication or drip:** given into a vein through a thin, soft tube called a cannula.
