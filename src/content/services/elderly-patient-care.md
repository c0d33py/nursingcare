Growing older or being confined to bed shouldn't mean losing comfort or dignity. Our caregivers (ayas) and assistant nurses help with personal hygiene, feeding and mobility, and with regular turning and repositioning to protect the skin from bed sores.

Just as importantly, they offer **patience and companionship**: a familiar face, a conversation over tea, help with a short walk. As needs change, care can be combined with nursing visits or doctor check-ups, all arranged by one team.

## A caregiver's day can include

- Help with washing, dressing and toileting, always respecting privacy
- Preparing and helping with meals, and making sure fluids are taken
- Reminders for medicines (given as prescribed)
- Gentle mobility, turning and positioning
- Companionship, conversation and keeping the family updated
