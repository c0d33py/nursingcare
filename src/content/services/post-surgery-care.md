The first days and weeks after surgery matter. Our team supports recovery at home with post-operative nursing, monitoring, medication support, dressing and wound care, and help moving safely.

We coordinate follow-up care with the family and, where needed, the treating doctor or hospital. Support steps down as recovery progresses, typically from shifts to daily visits to the occasional check-in, so you only ever pay for the care that's needed.

## Common reasons families call

- Hip or knee replacement, and fracture surgery
- Abdominal or gall bladder surgery
- Caesarean section recovery
- Heart procedures and longer hospital stays
