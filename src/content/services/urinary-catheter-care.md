Living with a urinary catheter needs careful, regular attention to stay comfortable and avoid infection. Our qualified staff insert and replace catheters at home, provide routine catheter care and monitor urine output.

Every procedure is carried out **privately and respectfully**, and you can ask for a staff member of the same gender. For many families, that alone makes the difference between dreading a hospital trip and getting it done calmly at home.

## Signs to call us about

- Little or no urine draining
- Fever, cloudy or strong-smelling urine
- Pain, leaking or bleeding around the catheter
