For elderly or unwell patients, getting to a clinic can be exhausting, or simply impossible. The journey, the parking, the waiting room: for someone who is frail, in pain or recovering, it can take a whole day out of them. Our doctors visit at home instead, to examine the patient, review their medicines and keep an eye on long-term conditions.

After a hospital stay, a home assessment helps make sure recovery is on track. The doctor checks how the patient is doing, looks at the discharge advice and medicines, and tells the family exactly what to watch for.

Where specialist input or hospital care is needed, we help coordinate referrals and keep the family informed at every step.

## When families usually call us

- An elderly parent who finds it hard to leave the house
- A routine review of blood pressure, diabetes or heart medicines
- A check-up in the days after coming home from hospital
- A second look when something "doesn't seem right" but isn't an emergency
