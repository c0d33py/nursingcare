A nasogastric (NG) tube is a thin, soft tube passed through the nose into the stomach. It gives food, fluids and medicine when a patient can't swallow safely, for example after a stroke or during a long illness.

Our qualified staff insert NG tubes at home, give tube feeds, look after the tube and watch the patient closely during and after feeding. We also show family members how to support feeding safely between visits, so you feel confident rather than anxious.

## Signs to call us about

- The tube looks like it has moved or come partly out
- Coughing, choking or breathlessness during a feed
- Vomiting, or a swollen, painful stomach
