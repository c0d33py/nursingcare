Wounds heal best when they're cleaned and dressed correctly and on time. Our staff provide wound care and dressing changes at home for surgical wounds, pressure sores (bed sores) and diabetic wounds, following appropriate infection-control practices.

We keep an eye on how the wound is healing and **let the family and doctor know if anything needs attention**. With bed sores especially, early care and regular repositioning make a big difference, and we'll show the family how to help.

## Signs to call us about

- Increasing redness, swelling, heat or pain
- New discharge, a bad smell or bleeding
- Fever, or the wound opening up
