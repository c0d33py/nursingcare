When a patient needs skilled care beyond what family members can safely provide, a qualified nurse makes all the difference. Our nurses provide bedside care, give medication as prescribed, carry out general nursing procedures and keep a close eye on the patient's condition.

Care can be arranged as **day shifts, night shifts or around the clock**. Our nurses work alongside the family, not instead of it. They explain what they're doing, answer questions and let you know promptly about any change in the patient's condition.

## Day, night or 24-hour nursing

- **Day shift:** medication, monitoring, dressings and help through the busiest part of the day.
- **Night shift:** an overnight watch, turning and repositioning, night-time medicines, and someone awake when it matters.
- **24-hour care:** two nurses on rotating 12-hour shifts, so the patient is never left without qualified support.
