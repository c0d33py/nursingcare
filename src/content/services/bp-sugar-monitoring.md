For people with diabetes, high blood pressure or heart and lung conditions, regular monitoring is one of the simplest ways to stay safe. Our staff check blood pressure, blood sugar, temperature, pulse, breathing rate and oxygen saturation (SpO₂) at home.

We record the readings and share them with the family, so everyone can see the trend and not just a single number. **Unusual readings are flagged** so the right action can be taken quickly, whether that's a call to the family, a medicine review or a doctor's visit.

## What the numbers mean

- **Blood pressure:** how hard the blood pushes against artery walls. High readings raise the risk of stroke and heart problems.
- **Blood sugar:** essential for people with diabetes. Both high and low readings matter.
- **SpO₂:** how much oxygen the blood is carrying, measured with a small finger clip.
