## About Heaven Nursing Care 24/7

Heaven Nursing Care 24/7 provides professional medical, nursing and personal healthcare services at home, allowing patients to receive quality care in the comfort, privacy and safety of their own homes.

We provide a complete range of healthcare professionals, including:

- Doctors
- Qualified Nurses
- Paramedical Staff
- Assistant Nurses
- Ayas / Caregivers

Our services are designed for elderly patients, bedridden patients, post-surgical patients, patients with chronic illnesses, dependent patients and anyone requiring professional healthcare support at home.

- General medical examination
- Follow-up consultation
- Elderly patient assessment
- Chronic disease monitoring
- Post-hospitalization assessment
- Medication review and medical advice

### 02. Professional Nursing Care

- Qualified nurse at home
- Day and night nursing care
- 24/7 nursing support
- Bedside patient care
- Post-operative nursing care
- Chronic illness care
- Elderly patient care
- Medication administration as prescribed

### 03. Injection & Medication Services

- IM injections
- Subcutaneous injections

### 04. BP, Sugar & Vital Signs Monitoring

- Blood Pressure (BP) checking
- Blood Sugar / Glucose checking
- Temperature monitoring
- Pulse monitoring
- Respiratory rate monitoring
- Oxygen saturation (SpO₂) monitoring
- Regular patient observation

### 05. NG Tube & Feeding Care

- NG tube insertion by qualified staff
- NG tube feeding
- Tube care and maintenance
- Feeding assistance
- Monitoring during feeding

### 06. Urinary Catheter Care

- Catheter insertion by qualified staff
- Catheter replacement
- Catheter care
- Urine-output monitoring
- Care for catheter-dependent patients

### 07. Wound & Dressing Care

- Wound care
- Dressing changes
- Post-surgical wound care
- Bed sore / pressure sore care
- Diabetic wound care
- Appropriate infection-control practices

### 08. Elderly & Patient Care

- Senior citizen care
- Bedridden patient care
- Personal hygiene assistance
- Feeding assistance
- Mobility assistance
- Turning and positioning
- Daily patient-care support
- Comfort and companionship

### 09. Post-Surgery & Recovery Care

- Post-operative nursing care
- Recovery monitoring
- Medication support
- Dressing and wound care
- Mobility assistance
- Follow-up care coordination

### 10. Paramedical & Medical Support

We provide appropriate paramedical and clinical support at home according to the patient's medical condition and requirements.
